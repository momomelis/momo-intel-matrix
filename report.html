Technology Assessment — Profusa Biosensor

Profusa's Lumee™ implantable biosensor platform represents a generational leap in continuous physiological monitoring. Unlike surface-contact wearables, the Profusa system uses a thread-like hydrogel sensor implanted just beneath the skin, providing laboratory-accurate continuous readings of oxygen saturation, glucose, lactate, and other biomarkers — without the noise and latency of optical skin sensors.

Core Technology Stack
  • Injectable hydrogel tissue-integrating biosensor (~5mm thread)
  • Continuous oxygen (SpO2) and multi-analyte monitoring
  • Near-field optical readout — no battery in implant
  • 2-year implant lifetime without replacement
  • Body Reader wearable device (external, rechargeable)
  • Cloud analytics and AI inference platform
Key Technical Advantages
  • Continuous real-time data vs. spot-check wearables
  • Sub-dermal accuracy: ±1.5% SpO2 (vs ±3-5% optical)
  • Works during exercise, sleep, water immersion
  • Biocompatible — no immune rejection in 2yr trials
  • Platform extensible to glucose, lactate, pH
  • Proven in DARPA field conditions (extreme environments)
